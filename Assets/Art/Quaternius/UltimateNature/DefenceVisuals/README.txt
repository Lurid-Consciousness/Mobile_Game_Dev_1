Visual-only arrangement for the eight existing WallDefence objects.

Mesh: normalized copy of Quaternius Ultimate Nature Pack WoodLog.fbx (CC0); original FBX and licence retained.
Materials: URP/Lit, using the source model colours.
Each wall has a LogVisuals child with eight horizontal logs and two upright supports. The child scale cancels the existing wall scale, including its negative Y scale.
Existing positions, rotations, scales, colliders and gameplay components are unchanged. Only the original cube MeshRenderers are disabled. No scripts, joints or Rigidbodies added.
To restore the old appearance, remove LogVisuals and re-enable the wall MeshRenderer.
