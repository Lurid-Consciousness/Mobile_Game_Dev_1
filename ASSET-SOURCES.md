# Woodland asset sources

## Quaternius - CC0

- Cube World Kit: https://quaternius.com/packs/cubeworldkit.html
- Raccoon listing: https://poly.pizza/m/xMmx6VQP3r
- Raccoon.fbx and AnimalAtlas.png under Assets/Art/WoodlandAnimals came from the creator's linked download. Idle/Walk clips use the local Raccoon.controller. Materials/import settings were adapted for this project.
- Existing Ultimate Nature models reused: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatenature.html

## ambientCG - CC0

- Grass004: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Grass004
- Ground037: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Ground037
- Downloaded 1K JPG color and OpenGL normal maps are under Assets/Art/EnvironmentTextures. Local URP materials apply them to ground/trails.
- License: https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/

## Local prototype artwork

Squirrel, bear, balloon possum, acorn detailing, log barriers and glider wing are locally assembled prototype artwork. WoodlandMotion provides basic procedural animation; raccoon clips come from its creator. No executable, physics library, sound or music package was installed for this pass.

## Credits presentation

The in-game credits distinguish Wael Al-Malki's design/programming, Codex assistance, the supplied boomerang model, locally assembled prototype characters, Kenney audio/input resources, Quaternius models/animations and ambientCG textures. No new downloaded asset was required for the powerup pass.
